Privacy Policy

srm-app is used only by its owner. It requests access to the owner's Google Calendar (calendar.events scope) solely to read existing events and create events the owner has agreed to.

Calendar data is processed on the owner's own computer, is not shared with or sold to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
